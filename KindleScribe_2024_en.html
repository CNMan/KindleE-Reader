Your Kindle is regularly updated with improvements to the reading experience. Have feedback? Please share it with the Kindle team by going to Contact Us under Settings and then Help & User Guides

Version 5.20.2.0.1 – October 2026

Here's what's new:

Ask This Book: In book search now includes Ask This Book, a conversational AI assistant that enables you to ask questions about your English-language Kindle book while you are reading it, without revealing spoilers.

"Auto-snap shapes" toggle in notebooks: Control whether holding at the end of a stroke snaps your hand-drawn shapes into clean geometric forms.

Performance improvements, bug fixes, and other general enhancements.

Version 5.20.1 – September 2026

Handwritten Sticky Notes (Fixed Layout): You can now add handwritten sticky notes in fixed layout eBooks or personal documents. Use the sticky note tool in the writing toolbar or select text and use the action menu.

Performance improvements, bug fixes, and other general enhancements.
